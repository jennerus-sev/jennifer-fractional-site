The challenge

Coverage is not the same as access.

Many Medicare Advantage and dual-eligible members have OTC benefits but aren't always sure what's covered, what they'll owe, or how to get the products they need.

The design opportunity was to make benefits easier to use in the way that works for the member, while coordinating plan requirements, customer care teams, technology, suppliers, and fulfillment.

Research insights

What we learned changed our approach.

Eight interviews with dual-eligible members and a broader member survey distinguished awareness barriers from the friction of actually completing an order.

Clarity before choice

Understanding benefit availability, covered products, and out-of-pocket costs helped address hesitation and checkout surprises.

Trust is personal

Helpful, knowledgeable customer care reps could give people confidence that guidance was legitimate and orders would be correct.

Make the next step easy

Simple offers, accessible shopping options, and reliable delivery mattered more than adding another complicated channel.

Outcomes

From research to a coordinated service launch.

Research informed a coordinated benefit service that connected customer care teams, member guidance, ordering, operational safeguards, and delivery in a successful launch.

Make the OTC benefit easier to use

Customer care reps made the benefit visible at signup, helped members find products they needed, and guided them through ordering. Orders could move to fulfillment once eligibility was verified.

Drive plan utilization

We engaged members right after signup by helping them start an OTC order before coverage took effect. Members who use their plan within the first 90 days are more likely to stay with that plan.

Extend the insurance carrier brand

Coordinated fulfillment and home delivery carried the plan’s service and messaging from the enrollment conversation to the member’s doorstep, with order support along the way.

Surface insight for the health plan

Aggregated search and ordering patterns gave the plan a clearer view of what members looked for and what they chose.
